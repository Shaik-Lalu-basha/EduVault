About Our Platform

Understanding what makes EduVault unique

What is EduVault?

EduVault is a comprehensive, secure educational platform designed specifically for B.Tech and M.Tech students. We understand the challenges students face in finding, accessing, and managing quality educational resources. Our platform solves this by providing a centralized, secure repository of academic materials.

With EduVault, students can access PDFs, lab manuals, simplified notes, and video tutorials all in one place - organized by year, branch, and subject. All resources are protected with advanced security features to maintain academic integrity.

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding principles that drive our platform

Our Vision

To create a world where quality engineering education is universally accessible and protected. We envision a future where every student has equal access to comprehensive learning resources without compromising on security and academic integrity.

Our Mission

To provide a secure, user-friendly platform that centralizes educational resources for graduate students. Our mission is to support academic excellence while protecting intellectual property rights through advanced security measures and watermarking.

Why Was EduVault Created?

The problems we sought to solve

Fragmented Resources

Students had to search across multiple platforms to find study materials. We created a single unified platform where all resources are organized and accessible.

Lack of Security

Educational content was easily downloadable and shareable, violating intellectual property. EduVault implements advanced security to protect academic materials.

Poor Organization

Most platforms lack proper categorization. We organized resources by year, semester, branch, and subject for easy navigation and discovery.

Academic Quality

Students couldn't find curated, quality resources. We handpicked and organized the best educational materials for maximum learning impact.

Our Security Commitment

Protecting your academic integrity

No Direct Downloads

PDFs are streamed directly to the viewer. Users cannot download files to their device.

Disabled Right-Click

Context menu is disabled to prevent unauthorized copying and extraction.

Screenshot Prevention

Print screen and keyboard shortcuts for screenshot are disabled.

Watermarking

Every viewed PDF is watermarked with the student's ID for tracking.

Blur on Tab Change

Content blurs when you switch tabs, preventing screen capture tools.

Developer Tools Blocked

F12, Ctrl+U, and other developer tools are disabled.
