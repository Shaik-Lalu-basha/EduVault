Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Is EduVault free to use?

Yes! EduVault is completely free for all students. We believe education should be accessible to everyone.

Can I download PDFs from EduVault?

No, for security and copyright protection reasons, direct downloads are not allowed. However, you can view, search, and print documents through our secure viewer.

Which engineering branches are covered?

We cover 12+ major engineering branches including CSE, ECE, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, and more for both B.Tech and M.Tech programs.

How often are new resources added?

We continuously update our platform with new PDFs, notes, and video tutorials. Check back regularly for fresh content!

How is my privacy protected?

We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols. Your personal information is never shared with third parties.

Can I request specific content?

Absolutely! Use the contact form above to request specific resources or subjects you'd like us to add.
